# RESILIQ (RSQ)

**RESILIQ — *Measure resilience the way capital measures risk.***

---

Every organisation that buys cyber insurance carries two versions of the truth.
One lives inside the business: the security team knows which endpoints are covered, which backups have been tested, which vendors are monitored. The other lives on a carrier's questionnaire, where that reality is reduced to a handful of checkboxes filled in under deadline pressure by people who would rather be doing anything else.
The gap between those two versions is where money disappears. A Chief Risk Officer at a mid-sized financial firm knows her defences are stronger than last year's quote suggests, but she cannot prove it in a format the underwriter will accept. So the carrier prices uncertainty instead of reality, and she pays for it. Meanwhile her team spends three weeks a year rebuilding evidence that already sits in their GRC platform, their endpoint tools, and their identity systems.

**RESILIQ closes that gap.**
It sits quietly between the systems that already know how resilient an organisation is and the insurers who need to believe it. When a renewal window opens, RESILIQ reaches into the tools the business already runs, pulls current control evidence, and maps it — deterministically, with every field traced back to its source — into the exact questionnaire the carrier uses. The CRO does not write a narrative. She reviews a gap report that tells her something genuinely useful: *your backup immutability is strong enough to move you into a better pricing tier, but your MFA coverage sits four points below the threshold this carrier rewards.* That is no longer a compliance chore. That is a capital allocation decision.
The same evidence does double duty. It goes out the door to the underwriter, and it stays inside as the foundation of the board's resilience dashboard. There is no "board version" and "insurance version" of the truth — just one auditable fabric, serving the CRO, the regulator, and the underwriter from the same source.
We are honest about what this takes. Insurance evidence cannot be invented by a language model. Every field is filled only from a verified source, every transformation is logged, every submission can be reproduced six months later when a carrier asks a hard question. The data governance is not decoration bolted onto a risk tool; it is the reason the output is trusted at all. And because RESILIQ is built to CMMI Level 5, the platform's own engineering discipline becomes part of its credibility — the rare vendor that can show a regulated buyer its own process performance evidence.
The commercial logic is deliberately unglamorous. Start with the evidence pack, because a broker can see the premium savings and the CRO can see the hours returned. Then deepen into the internal decision platform, because once the evidence is trusted externally, nobody wants to maintain a second system internally.

**RESILIQ does not sell resilience as a virtue. It sells resilience as a measurable asset; and then proves it, line by line, to the people who price it.**


##  Comprehensive Module Taxonomy

The module architecture is organised into nine domains. Every module is designed to be **independently deployable but natively interoperable** through the shared evidence graph, the ontology layer, and the RBAC model. The CMMI Level 5 process management domain is cross-cutting: it instruments the engineering and operation of every other module, not a standalone product feature.
